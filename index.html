Four problems, worked end to end

JDG Jewelers

Business Strategy & Growth Intern

2026

Finding the 92% in an $8,000 ad budget

Challenge

JDG was running Meta ad campaigns without a clear read on whether the spend was actually driving sales.

Approach

I audited 116 Meta campaigns end to end, categorizing each by objective and tracing spend against measurable outcomes. Alongside that I benchmarked 4 regional competitors and analyzed 500+ customer reviews to map gaps in pricing, digital presence and after-sale experience.

Findings

92% of the $8,000+ monthly budget, roughly $7,400, was going toward engagement-objective campaigns generating likes and clicks with no measurable path to a sale.

Outcome

I built a reallocation plan redirecting spend toward conversion-driven campaigns, paired with differentiation recommendations centered on customer experience and brand trust, and presented the full proposal directly to the owner.

JDG Jewelers

Campaign design and channel strategy

2026

A bilingual rebrand, in two voices

Challenge

JDG’s messaging leaned discount-first, which undercut the premium positioning ownership wanted for an end-of-summer push.

Approach

I designed and led a bilingual English and Spanish campaign from the ground up: a custom tiered promotional structure, a channel strategy across the brand’s platforms, and original print collateral.

Outcome

The campaign moved JDG from discount-first to a premium, trust-first brand voice, in both languages, for a bilingual customer base the discount framing was not fully serving.
