JDG Jewelers

Meta ad audit, pro bono

2026

Finding the 92% in an $8,000+ ad budget

Challenge

JDG was running Meta ads without a clear read on whether the spend was driving sales.

Approach

I audited 116 Meta ads, sorted each one by objective, and traced spend against measurable outcomes.

Findings

92% of an $8,000+ budget, about $7,400, was going to engagement-objective ads producing likes and clicks with no measurable sales.

Outcome

I built a plan to redirect that spend toward conversion campaigns and presented it to the owner.
